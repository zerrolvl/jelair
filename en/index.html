Fragrance pyramid

A scent opens in three stages

  1. 0–15 minutes

    Top notes

    The first impression. Light and fleeting: citrus, green leaves, spices. They appear fast and fade fast.

  2. 15 minutes – 4 hours

    Heart notes

    The character of the scent. Flowers, fruit and spices open fully at this stage.

  3. After 4 hours

    Base notes

    The trail that stays longest on skin. Woods, musk, amber and vanilla give the scent depth.

Guide

Make your scent last longer

Moisturise first

Scent lasts longer on moisturised skin. Apply cream, then layer the scent on top.

On pulse points

Wrists, neck and the inside of the elbows. Warm skin opens the scent better.

Don’t rub

Rubbing breaks down the scent and shortens how long it lasts. Let it dry on its own.

Hair too

A perfumed hair spray carries the scent all day and protects from dryer and iron heat.
